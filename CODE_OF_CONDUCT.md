# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want DevRadar to be a welcoming place for contributors of all backgrounds.
Harassment, discrimination, and spam of the review queue or community channels
are not welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, PRs, and reviews.
- Assume good intent; disagree about ideas, not people.
- Prefer small, well-scoped contributions over drive-by noise.
- Do not submit malicious or knowingly false catalogue data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, trolling, or hate speech.
- Publishing others’ private information without consent.
- Flooding the admin review queue or alert system with abuse.
- Sharing secrets, stolen data, or exploit details in public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or close comments, reject contributions, block accounts,
or revoke abusive admin access. Serious security issues should follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issues.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces (GitHub issues, PRs, discussions)
and when representing the project in public.

## Attribution

Adapted from common open-source norms and the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) spirit, kept short for a small project.
